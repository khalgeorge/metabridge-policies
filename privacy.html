Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Facebook account to MetaBridge, we collect your Facebook User ID, name, email address, and the access tokens required to manage your Facebook Pages on your behalf. We do not collect or store your Facebook password.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the MetaBridge service — scheduling and publishing posts to your Facebook Pages. Your access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and are never shared with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in an encrypted database. Access tokens are encrypted at rest and only decrypted in isolated background workers during publishing operations.

4. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of all your personal data at any time. Upon request, all your data including access tokens and connected accounts will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Facebook users can request data deletion by visiting: MetaBridge Data Deletion Page

5. Third-Party Services

MetaBridge uses the Facebook Graph API to publish content on your behalf. Your use of Facebook is subject to Facebook's Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at the email address associated with your MetaBridge account.
