Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of your MetaBridge data

What data does MetaBridge store?

MetaBridge stores your Facebook User ID, name, email address, Facebook Page access tokens (encrypted), and any posts scheduled through our platform.

How to delete your data

Option 1 – Delete through Facebook

  1. Go to your Facebook App Settings
  2. Find MetaBridge (or TheBridge) in the list
  3. Click Remove
  4. Facebook will automatically send us a deletion request

Option 2 – Contact us directly

  1. Log in to your MetaBridge account
  2. Contact us via the email on your account requesting deletion
  3. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days

What happens after deletion?

Once your data is deleted, MetaBridge will no longer be able to publish to your Facebook Pages. All stored access tokens, account information, and scheduled posts are permanently and irreversibly removed.

Questions?

For questions about your data, contact us at the email associated with your MetaBridge account.
